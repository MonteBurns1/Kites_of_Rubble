# 🕊️ Kites of Rubble | طائرات من ركام  
**An informative graphic design project about the suffering, resilience, and lost innocence of children in Gaza.**

---

## 💡 Project Concept  
**Kites of Rubble** is a graduation project that sheds light on the human suffering and daily realities of the people in Gaza amid the ongoing genocide.  
The project documents destruction, displacement, and endurance — using **kites as a visual metaphor** for hope and freedom rising above the ruins.

It explores how **art, design, and visual storytelling** can serve as humanitarian awareness tools, transforming real stories and data into compelling graphics and educational visuals.

The project includes:  
- A cohesive **visual identity** symbolizing resilience and hope.  
- **Logo and branding** inspired by the Palestinian flag and keffiyeh patterns.  
- **Informational posters** and **social media visuals** highlighting the crisis.  
- **Printed awareness materials** for exhibitions and campaigns.

## 🗂️ Work Plan  

### 1. Research & Documentation  
- Study the humanitarian situation and children’s experiences in Gaza.  
- Gather visual references and verified data from global sources (UN, UNICEF, etc.).  
- Develop the project’s emotional and symbolic direction.

### 2. Visual Identity  
- Design a logo inspired by the **Palestinian flag** and **keffiyeh pattern**.  
- Define color palette, typography, and consistent visual elements.  
- Establish design tone: hopeful yet somber.

### 3. Awareness Design  
- Posters, infographics, and exhibition-ready prints.  
- Digital campaign visuals for online use.  
- Incorporate bilingual presentation (English + Arabic).

### 4. Presentation & Documentation  
- Prepare GitHub documentation, slides, and portfolio-ready visuals.  
- Final review for accuracy, tone, and ethical representation.
